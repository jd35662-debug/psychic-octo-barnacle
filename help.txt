Undertail-Style Mini RPG
Update 0.0.1

What we have now
- A simple top-down playable demo in a single page web app.
- Movement controls: WASD or Arrow Keys.
- Interact with E.

Rooms and progression
- First room (courtyard):
  - Large giant door at the top.
  - Small side door on the right that leads to training grounds.
- Training grounds:
  - Three class pads (Warrior, Mage, Rogue).
  - Stand on a class pad and press E to select class.
  - Class selection adds a small colored square above the player.

Doors and keys
- The giant door in the first room requires "Key ???".
- Using "Key ???" unlocks the giant door and consumes the key.

Inventory and items
- Inventory is visible in the HUD.
- Item table lists all objects that can appear in inventory.
- Includes generic keys and quest items.
- "Key ???" is always shown in grey.

Mode slider
- Dev mode: shows the item table.
- Customers mode: hides the item table.

Visual style
- Uses simple stand-in color textures for map, doors, pads, and player.
